MOH Singapore Clinical Haze Advisory

Official Ministry of Health Guidelines by Particulate Band & Population Cohort

Band 1 (Normal 0–55 µg/m³ / PSI 0–50)

Normal Activity
  • Healthy Persons: Normal outdoor activities and physical exercise.
  • Elderly, Pregnant & Children: Normal outdoor activities.
  • Chronic Lung / Heart Diseases: Normal outdoor activities.

Band 2 (Elevated 56–150 µg/m³ / PSI 51–100)

Moderate Vigilance
  • Healthy Persons: Normal outdoor activities; reduce prolonged intense exertion.
  • Elderly, Pregnant & Children: Reduce sustained intense outdoor exertion.
  • Chronic Lung / Heart Diseases: Avoid strenuous physical exertion; have medication readily available.

Band 3 & 4 (High & Very High ≥151 µg/m³ / PSI >100)

Restricted Activity
  • Healthy Persons: Minimize outdoor activities and strenuous physical exertion.
  • Elderly, Pregnant & Children: Stay indoors with windows closed and particulate air filters activated.
  • Chronic Lung / Heart Diseases: Avoid all outdoor exposure. Seek prompt medical care if experiencing respiratory distress.
